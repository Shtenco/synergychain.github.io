02 · BASE MAINNET PLANE

Base-side custody, execution и prefunded settlement.

BaseBridgeVault

Lock canonical WETH/USDC; issue corresponding bridge liability; track accruedFees separately; validator quorum, replay protection and pause.

BaseArbExecutor

External USDC → WETH route through Base market, then WETH into bridge custody. Slippage guard remains explicit.

PrefundedSettlementVault

Advances real Base USDC against one-use quote/ticket while internal settlement catches up later. Receivables are explicit and capped.

BaseInventoryCycleAdapter

Closes each Base-side step back into USDC so sequential cycles can reuse a bounded flash principal.

USDC inventory ≥ max planned outstanding receivables + safety buffer
outstandingReceivable ≤ maxOutstandingReceivable
03 · WHY PREFUNDING EXISTS

Flash loan не отменяет cross-chain latency.

Что делает flash liquidity

Даёт атомарный Base-side principal и позволяет последовательно закрывать несколько внешних шагов в callback, если каждый step возвращается в canonical Base USDC.

flashAmount = max(step.amountIn), not Σ amountIn for sequentially closed steps
Что flash liquidity НЕ делает

Aave principal не может ждать asynchronous bridge/relayer. Поэтому immediate Base repayment требует заранее существующий USDC inventory, который временно становится receivable к internal settlement.

settlement inventory is working capital, not generated profit

05 · INTERNAL ATOMIC CREDIT LP

Временная ликвидность обязана исчезнуть в той же транзакции.

creditMint WETH/USDC→ADDLP→QE / settlement calls→removeLP→burn principals→burn temporary QE share→residual → treasury

AtomicCreditLiquidityManager

No separately callable open-position state. Any unwind shortfall reverts the entire EVM transaction, including credit mint.

Principal invariant

Temporary WETH and USDC principal is minted under a dedicated creditController and burned before return.

outstandingCreditMint(WETH)=0
outstandingCreditMint(USDC)=0

QE neutralization

A temporary LP is not allowed to monetize timer-QE produced while it is open. Its pro-rata QE share is burned and credit restored for future timer ticks.

QE_burn = α × QE_delta
06 · TIMER QE AND BACKED CREDIT

QE решает таймер. Credit решает платёжеспособность.

Funding credit

real Base USDC→BaseBridgeVault→liability / QE credit→relay→availableCredit

Base-side contract physically holds the settlement asset; internal timer-QE may mint only within the communicated credit ceiling.

Organic fees → future QE backing

bridge volume→USDC fees→accruedFees→governance conversion→new QE credit

Это один из реальных mechanisms, как внешняя выручка может постепенно обеспечивать следующий policy cycle.

08 · ACCOUNTING SEPARATION

Четыре капитала нельзя смешивать в одну цифру.

Grid capital

Permanent market/liquidity capital.

Bridge reserve / QE backing

Assets supporting bridge claims and authorized QE credit.

Flash principal

Borrowed transient Base liquidity; liability disappears only after repayment + premium.

Settlement inventory

Prefunded working capital against explicit receivables; not distributable while encumbered.

09 · NEGATIVE EVIDENCE

Почему мы храним неудобные результаты.

TVL illusion

Large local TVL growth can coexist with even larger required new canonical backing. Gross internal value is not self-financed wealth.

QT asymmetry

Buyback/burn consumes real treasury. Contraction is not a free inverse of mint.

Temporary LP QE capture

Without pro-rata QE neutralization, temporary flash LP can withdraw policy issuance and make it look like flash profit. The accounting fix burns that share.
