02 · WHY ATOMIC

Нет «позиции, которую потом кто-нибудь закроет».

Отдельное open-position state создаёт operational and governance risk. Atomic design делает incomplete unwind невозможным как successful state: любой shortfall reverts всю transaction, включая initial credit mint.

No stranded debt

Temporary credit is not left outstanding across blocks.

No discretionary rescue

Success does not depend on a future keeper choosing to repay.

State rollback

If principal cannot be burned, the entire transaction returns to pre-credit state.

03 · POLICY ISSUANCE NEUTRALIZATION

Temporary LP не должен забирать QE себе.

Если timer-QE increases a pool reserve while temporary LP owns share α of liquidity, LP withdrawal would mechanically include α of policy issuance. Без correction это выглядит как flash strategy profit.

αtemporary liquidity share

Temporary LP / total LP during the policy event.

ΔQEpolicy reserve increase

New issuance added while temporary position exists.

α·ΔQEneutralized share

Burn/reclassify before any residual is considered strategy return.

ResidualProfitCandidate = WithdrawnAssets − CreditPrincipal − PolicyShareNeutralization − Fees/Costs
04 · PERMISSIONS

Credit mint — очень узкое право.

Dedicated creditController may mint only approved temporary assets, only inside batch, under caps. It does not gain general token mint authority.

PermissionRule
creditMintOnly approved asset, amount cap, atomic context.
ADDLPOnly approved pool/router, min liquidity / slippage checks.

05 · FAILURE MODES

Главная атака — выдать borrowed/policy value за profit.

Principal leakage

Credit not fully burned.

QE capture

Temporary LP exits with policy issuance.

Slippage unwind

LP removal recovers insufficient principal.

Unapproved call

Batch uses temporary mint outside intended scope.

06 · ATOMIC TRUTH

Если кредит исчезает к концу транзакции,
это всё равно не бесплатный капитал.

Он меняет path capacity and temporary market state. Любой residual должен пройти conservation/accounting checks, особенно если во время position произошла policy issuance.

07 · PROMOTION GATE

L4 требует adversarial fork integration.

Tests: insufficient unwind, policy event before/after addLP, multi-step batch, rounding dust, fee-on-transfer behavior where relevant, reentrancy, unauthorized adapter, and invariant `outstandingCreditMint==0` across all successful exits.
