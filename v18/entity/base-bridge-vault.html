04 · SECURITY MODEL

Custody risk — economic risk.

Compromised bridge can invalidate every downstream accounting assumption. Therefore security properties are part of the economic passport.

Replay protection

One deposit/proof cannot mint twice.

Validator / attestation quorum

Proof threshold and signer rotation are explicit.

Pause

New mint/unlock can be halted on mismatch.

Per-asset / route caps

Bound maximum exposure.

Role separation

Fee withdrawal, mint attestation and emergency control are separate permissions.

Upgrade boundary

Upgrade cannot silently rewrite backing semantics.

Observability

Coverage, pending events and failed relays are visible.

Recovery

Incident mode defines claim reconciliation before normal operation.
