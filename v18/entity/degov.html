01 · GOVERNANCE OBJECTS

Не всё голосуется одинаково.

DEGOV классифицирует decisions by impact. Product experiment, operational budget, Treasury risk cap and constitutional backing rule require different thresholds.

TierExamplesTypical barrier
OperationalRoutine spend, product campaign, maintenanceDelegated role / budget owner

EmergencyPause exploit routeFast narrow authority; slow restoration
02 · RIGHTS SEPARATION

Vote ≠ ownership ≠ cashflow ≠ execution key.

Одна из фундаментальных защит: governance token or membership cannot silently imply ownership of personal assets or unrestricted signing authority.

Ownership right

Кому принадлежит asset/IP/share?

Cashflow right

Кто получает surplus/fee/dividend and under what formula?

Governance right

Кто участвует в changing rules/budgets?

Execution permission

Кто/какой contract может подписать конкретную state transition?

03 · DELEGATION

Делегируется мандат, не власть навсегда.

Delegation object should contain scope, max amount/risk, expiry, allowed contracts/actions and recall path.

Mandate = {scope, entities, methods, budget, maxLoss, start, expiry, auditRequirements, recallRule}

05 · ANTI-CAPTURE

Децентрализация проверяется при атаке на власть.

Timelocks

High-impact changes can be reviewed/challenged before execution.

Recall / expiration

Delegated authority does not live forever.

Conflict handling

Direct beneficiaries disclose/conflict-adjust voting participation.

Multi-role separation

Proposal, execution, audit and custody are not one key.

Exit rights

Minority/individual can exit under known settlement rules.

Constitutional floor

Ordinary majority cannot silently remove core backing/ownership rights.

06 · GOVERNANCE ILLUSION

Тысяча голосов может быть декорацией,
если один ключ исполняет всё.

V18 оценивает реальную власть по capabilities, custody, upgrade rights and execution paths — не по количеству кнопок Vote.

07 · DEPENDENCIES / EVIDENCE

DEGOV связан с Treasury и contracts.

It governs mandates for Treasury/policy execution and bounds NEXUS proposals. Future L2/L3 evidence requires executable governance schemas, tests of delegation/recall/timelock and adversarial capture simulations.
