03 · ROUTING

Лучший route — не только самый дешёвый.

Routing objective combines cost, finality, liquidity, compliance, failure probability and user/merchant SLA.

Cost

Network/processor/bridge/FX fee.

Finality

How and when merchant can treat payment as settled.

Reliability

Historical failure/retry rate.

Liquidity

Capacity without excessive slippage/delay.

Compliance

Allowed jurisdiction/asset/merchant path.

Fraud / dispute risk

Expected loss/chargeback exposure.

Privacy

Collect only necessary transaction/customer data under rules.

Fallback

Alternative route if primary rail fails.

05 · FAILURE MODES

Платёжная система ломается на несогласованности state.

Double settlement

Merchant/customer state diverges.

Customer funds treated as revenue

Hidden insolvency.

Route failure

Funds stuck / retry duplication.

Reserve underfunding

Refund/chargeback obligations exceed buffer.
