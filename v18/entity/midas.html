03 · TREASURY ACCOUNTING

Equity curve становится Treasury value только после realization.

Gross strategy P&L

Closed trade result before fees/funding/operational costs.

Net market P&L

Gross − commissions − spread/slippage − swap/funding − direct execution costs.

Transferable surplus

Net P&L minus risk/loss reserve and capital required to keep strategy operating.

TreasuryPnL = ClosedExternalPnL − TradingCosts − Funding − ExecutionCosts − RequiredLossProvision
04 · RISK CONTRACT

Edge без ограничения риска — не продукт.

Entity passport must expose capital allocation, max portfolio loss/drawdown budget, symbol/counterparty concentration, model uncertainty, position size policy, stop policy and emergency disable.

Capital cap

Maximum Treasury/risk capital allocated to MIDAS.

Portfolio risk

Aggregate exposure across symbols/models.

Model uncertainty

Confidence/dispersion affects admission and sizing.

Execution kill switch

Disable live orders independently of analytics.

06 · MARKET ILLUSION

Backtest показывает возможный edge.
Cash появляется только после внешнего fill и close.

Поэтому MIDAS никогда не финансирует Financial OS «своей прогнозной доходностью». Только реальный realized external result проходит Source-of-Funds Gate.

07 · PROMOTION GATE

До L6 нужны live economics и operations.

Live closed-trade history, execution cost attribution, capital capacity, incident/kill-switch evidence, independent audit of risk controls and stable runtime.
