01 · WHY IT EXISTS

Atomic repayment и cross-chain settlement живут в разном времени.

Flash loan callback должен вернуть principal в той же transaction. Bridge/relayer/internal settlement может завершиться позже. Prefunded inventory финансирует этот временной разрыв.

Immediate payout does not remove latency; it converts latency into a bounded receivable against prefunded working capital.

03 · BALANCE SHEET

Inventory и receivable не взаимозаменяемы.

USDC inventory

Canonical liquid asset physically available for approved advances.

Outstanding receivable

Claim against unfinished internal settlement. Carries delay/default risk.

Free surplus

Zero by default from merely advancing/recovering principal; only explicit fee/spread after reconciliation may become profit.

AvailableAdvanceCapacity = min(USDCInventory − SafetyBuffer, MaxOutstandingReceivable − OutstandingReceivable)
04 · RECEIVABLE RISK

Чем старше receivable, тем меньше capacity.

Age is economic state. Delayed internal settlement should reduce new quote limits and eventually pause the route.

StateMeaningAction
freshWithin expected settlement windowNormal monitoring

05 · DEPENDENCIES

Prefunding работает только после admission.

SettlementRouter

Claim eligibility/backing must already be valid; prefunding accelerates payout timing only.

BaseInventoryCycleAdapter

Closes Base-side execution into USDC and interfaces with quote/advance flow.

Liability / receivable registry

Outstanding advance remains visible until actual canonical settlement closes it.

06 · FAILURE MODES

Главная опасность — притвориться банком без резерва.

Unlimited receivable

Advances exceed inventory/risk capacity.

Replay ticket

Same quote paid multiple times.

Stale quote

Advance uses outdated price/fee state.

Hidden aging

Old receivables remain nominally valued as cash-equivalent.

07 · TIME ILLUSION

Prefunding делает settlement мгновенным для пользователя.
Но риск никуда не исчезает.

Он перемещается из latency в working-capital exposure. Поэтому UX speed обязательно связан с receivable accounting and caps.

08 · EVIDENCE / PROMOTION

L4 требует real asynchronous integration test.

Quote replay, expiry, partial return, vault exhaustion, aged settlement, delayed relayer and exact reconciliation must be tested against real/fork semantics.
