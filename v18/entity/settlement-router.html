SettlementRouter

Граница между внутренним monetary claim и канонической внешней платёжеспособностью. Его задача — не «обменять SYUSD на USDC любой ценой», а допустить settlement только в пределах реального backing, eligibility и liquidity.

01 · PURPOSE

Не дать internal mint стать hidden bridge debt.

Если SYUSD/другой внутренний claim можно бесконечно конвертировать в canonical USDC без backing gate, любой QE автоматически превращается в внешний liability. SettlementRouter разрывает эту ложную эквивалентность.

USDCxOut ≤ AvailableCanonicalBacking
Payout ≤ EligibleClaim ∩ AvailableBacking ∩ RouteLiquidity ∩ PolicyLimit

03 · ECONOMIC ROLE

Router не создаёт revenue. Он ограничивает payout.

Money role: `settlement`. Source-of-funds должен существовать вне Router: reserve, product revenue, external buyer, returned settlement inventory или другое canonical asset source.

Source of funds

None intrinsic. Router consumes available canonical backing/liquidity.

Liabilities

May close internal claim and create/settle canonical claim; must update Liability Registry.

Reserves

Cannot spend below mandatory backing/safety floors.

05 · FAILURE MODES

Правильный Router умеет сказать «нет».

Unlimited payout

Internal mint drains canonical reserve.

Stale backing

Uses outdated reserve/claim data.

Double settlement

Same claim redeemed more than once.

Priority inversion

Later claim consumes reserve required by senior/encumbered obligations.

06 · GOVERNANCE

Какие параметры можно менять.

Accepted claim types, per-route cap, reserve haircut, expiry, fee and emergency pause can be governance variables. The backing invariant itself should be constitutional/high-barrier, not ordinary UI configuration.

Invariant tier: backing/claim conservation → highest governance barrier
07 · EVIDENCE & PROVENANCE

Почему эта сущность появилась.

V16 recovered architecture explicitly separated SYUSD from USDCx and introduced `SettlementRouter` so internal policy money could reach native Base USDC only against available canonical backing. V17 preserved and expanded that concept with prefunded settlement and explicit receivables. V18 promotes it to a first-class protocol entity.
