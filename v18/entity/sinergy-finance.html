01 · CORE LEDGER

Личный капитал — баланс, не остаток карты.

03 · CAPITAL HISTORY

Точка сегодня важна.
Траектория важнее.

Historical net worth lets user see peak/drawdown/recovery, separate real savings from market repricing and understand which events created or destroyed capital.

04 · HOUSEHOLD SOLVENCY

Личная версия Financial OS constraints.

Liquid reserve

How many months/obligations can be covered without forced sale.

Debt service

Payment burden relative to income/free cashflow.

Currency exposure

Liabilities and assets in different currencies.

Concentration

Share of net worth in one illiquid/high-risk asset.

05 · PRIVACY / OWNERSHIP

Данные о человеке принадлежат не DAO по умолчанию.

Household data is especially sensitive. Participation in collective economy does not imply public exposure of balances, debts or transactions. Aggregate/zero-knowledge/selective disclosure patterns should be preferred where system-level proof is needed.

Personal financial data → owner-controlled; collective analytics → minimum necessary, explicitly authorized aggregation/proof.
