01 · RESPONSIBILITY

02 · AFFORDABILITY

Максимальная policy — не максимальный gradient.

Recovered simulator estimated bridge support produced by a candidate gradient and searched for the largest gradient whose support requirement fit available Treasury.

G_applied = max{g ∈ [1,G_requested] : RequiredBridgeSupport(g) ≤ AvailablePolicyBudget}

RequiredBridgeSupport(g)

Expected payout/bridge shortfall after QE-induced state and optimal clearing/arbitrage assumptions.

AvailablePolicyBudget

Authorized Treasury/credit excluding mandatory reserves and unrelated commitments.

Fallback

`reduce` lowers gradient; `stop` rejects cycle if even minimum safe action is unavailable.

05 · FAILURE MODES

Контроллер опасен, если оптимизирует только цену.

Stale oracle

Policy reacts to obsolete external reference.

Unlimited credit

Backing discipline disappears.

Ignoring liabilities

Gross Treasury appears available while claims consume it.

Price-only objective

Internal target achieved at cost of external NAV/solvency.

06 · POLICY TRUTH

Контроллер может двигать цену.
Он не может отменить conservation of value.

Любой payout, buyback or bridge support after policy must still be financed by real assets, explicit liabilities or authorized risk capital.

07 · EVIDENCE / PROMOTION

Для L4 нужен fork/testnet state trace.
